Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard – Excel

Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based **Sales Performance and Profitability Dashboard** developed to analyze business sales data and generate meaningful business insights.

The project uses **500 sales transactions from 2025** and focuses on sales performance, profitability, regional performance, product performance, monthly trends, and customer segments.

The goal of this project is to transform raw sales data into a clear and easy-to-understand dashboard that can support data-driven business decisions.

Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Track key business performance indicators
- Compare sales performance across regions
- Identify top and low-performing products
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare customer segment performance
- Identify products contributing the most to profit
- Present findings through a business dashboard

Dashboard Analysis
Sales by Region

Regional sales are compared to identify the strongest and weakest performing regions.
Key Insight:
North generated the highest regional sales, while West recorded the lowest.

Sales by Product
Product-level sales performance is analyzed to identify the products generating the highest revenue.

Key Insight: 
Laptop was the top-selling product, followed by Tablet.

Monthly Sales Trend
Monthly sales are visualized to understand changes in sales performance throughout 2025.

Key Insight:
April recorded the highest monthly sales, while March recorded the lowest.

Customer Segment Performance
Sales are analyzed across:
- Small Business
- Consumer
- Corporate

Key Insight:
Small Business generated the highest sales among the customer segments.

Profit by Product
Product profitability is analyzed by comparing sales, cost, and profit.

Key Insight: 
Laptop generated the highest profit, while Mouse generated the lowest profit.

Key Business Insights
- **North** generated the highest regional sales.
- **Laptop** was the top-selling product and highest profit contributor.
- **April** recorded the highest monthly sales.
- **Small Business** generated the highest sales among customer segments.
- The overall **profit margin was 28.44%**.
